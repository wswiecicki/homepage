Designed a CI/CD/CT solution for dynamic AEM content deployment to GCP, together with an extensive testing suite (performance, visual, functional, accessibility, Web Vitals, component, unit). The framework allowed the design and creation teams to securily and easily deploy changes to production environment in a timely fashion, preventing defects and issues from being published in an interactive manner.

Comprehensive CI/CD solutions for multiple complex web platforms were created using Cypress, Vitest, visual testing and Github Actions.

Multilayered automated quality gates were put in place to secure the delivery pipeline and prevent defects from progressing to production.

The use of model-based testing with automatic shrinking combined with visual testing allowed to increase the confidence in the product and drastically shortened the delivery time necessary.

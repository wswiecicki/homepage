I'm a strong believer in using cutting-edge, performant, and maintainable solutions - and in continuously evolving engineering practices to support long-term growth and innovation.
I believe quality is not just a checkbox or a final phase - it must be embedded from the ground up. That means starting with quality in mind during architecture planning and system design. I find that broad technical knowledge - from infrastructure to code - is essential for identifying risks early and ensuring sustainable, testable systems.

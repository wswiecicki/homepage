My approach combines strong technical knowledge, strategic thinking, and a sharp focus on delivery. I believe quality is not just a phase - it's embedded into the entire development process. I advocate for shift-left testing, test automation as code, and tight integration of QA into CI/CD pipelines. My goal is always the same: to ship high-quality, high-value software that scales with the business.
